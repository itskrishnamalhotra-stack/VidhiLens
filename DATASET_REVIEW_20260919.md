## Result

None of these sources supplies a ready, verified set of ordinary citizen complaints mapped to the BNS **and** IT Act. A larger document or legal-Q&A corpus would not resolve the remaining need for natural complaint narratives, valid section labels, hard negatives, missing-fact questions, and independent tests. We should keep source material separate from approved training examples and official-law records. No rows from the three newly examined repositories were added to the training export by this review.

## GSMS follow-up screen

The pinned 2,148-row BNS Q&A file was screened by [`screen_gsms_bns_qa.py`](scripts/screen_gsms_bns_qa.py). It checked source hashes, schema, six Q&A types per section, section/title mapping, comparison with the master law text, citations, duplicates, and the BNS 106(2) commencement exception. **2,131 passed these automated checks; 17 were held.** We selected **1,422** explanatory, element, and exception Q&A rows for auxiliary research training. The other 709 passing scenario/consequence rows remain research-only because sampled answers included overconfident or speculative wording. The current [`mixed-source-v17-complaint-gsms-research`](exports/mixed-source-v17-complaint-gsms-research/manifest.json) combines 4,523 complaint train rows with 1,422 GSMS auxiliary train rows; the complaint-only bundle is unchanged. The added GSMS rows are explicitly marked `automated_screen_pass_not_semantically_certified`, not gold complaint labels. Master DB reproduction and Part 2 checks passed.

## Verification notes

- Hugging Face repository metadata, publisher README/statistics, dataset-server rows/size API, and pinned raw JSONL samples were checked on 19 September 2026.
- GovIntel's 12,859/1,421 split counts were confirmed by streaming both raw files. The first-person cue count used a narrow English phrase regex; it is only evidence that the corpus is dominated by formal legal questions, not a final classification of every row.
- The Abby count discrepancy is internal to the published repository: the actual dataset-server size is 35,845 rows, matching `dataset_info.json`, while README says 35,851. The representative first rows also show unrelated subject matter and extraction artifacts.
- The GSMS repositories were created in June 2026. At this check, Hugging Face metadata showed 229 downloads/1 like for Q&A and 490 downloads/1 like for structured sections. These popularity measures are not an accuracy audit. [India Code's BNS text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf) and the [MHA BNS commencement notification](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/BhartiyaNyayaSanhita_24022024.pdf) record the section 106(2) exception. A later commencement would require a fresh official notification check before treating the subsection as operative.
