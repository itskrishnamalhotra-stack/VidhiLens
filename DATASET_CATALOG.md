# Dataset research for BNS + IT complaint assistant

Research checked 16–17 September 2026; data status updated 17 September 2026. Dataset claims are taken from their publishers' cards or repositories. A publisher's label is **not** a verified BNS/IT legal conclusion. Store licence, source URL, exact downloaded version/hash, and privacy review before importing examples. The additional GitHub and Kaggle search is in `PUBLIC_DATA_SHORTLIST.md`; the canonical local data layout is in `DATA_INVENTORY.md`.

## Data rule

No reliable, openly accessible, already reviewed corpus of everyday complaint text directly paired with current BNS **and** IT Act provisions was confirmed in this search. The project therefore needs its own small, reviewed scenario set. Use public datasets only after checking source, licence, privacy, legal mapping, and label quality. Record each scenario's origin and put near duplicates in the same train/dev/test group.

The first six downloaded public files are pinned by repository revision and SHA-256 in `external_candidates_manifest.json`; their 6,681 rows are in the master database. The separately downloaded LeSICiN text files are under `external_candidates/lesicin_6053791/`, with Zenodo checksums and SHA-256 values in `screening_manifest.json`. They remain **outside the master training tables** and are not counted in the 3,022 current trainer records. The expanded `data_drafts/review_queue.jsonl` contains 57 project-created fictional drafts plus all 104 unique usable in-scope complaints from the original BNS triage file. Its larger 161-row size is a screening pool, not 161 approved labels.

The additional eight acquired files are pinned in `additional_candidates_manifest.json`; their 10,892 rows are also indexed in the same master database, making 17,573 indexed public candidates in total. They add **zero approved training rows**. The full source-by-source screen and the 28-row Nyaya v3 review shortlist are documented in `ADDITIONAL_DATASET_AUDIT_20260917.md`.

## Repair rule for public candidates

Repair a narrow problem only when the source can be traced and the corrected row can be reviewed: normalize malformed JSON/schema, remove duplicates and personal details, correct a stale section against official text, or rewrite an awkward synthetic complaint while retaining provenance. Keep the original row ID and a before/after note. Reject a row when the underlying facts are absent, the permission/licence is unclear, the legal conclusion cannot be supported, or the content may expose a real person. Never silently transform IPC labels into BNS labels or call an AI rewrite a human-reviewed example.

## Current reviewed data batch

The current master has 3,084 accepted scenarios: 324 individually AI-reviewed and 2,760 silver synthetic variants from 115 fact-pattern families. The limited 19-section pilot has 3,022 train SFT records, 24 development records and 80 sealed-test scenarios. LeSICiN adds zero approved training rows at this point. Treat IT Act 66E as held until the official wording conflict is resolved. Check consent, dishonest intent, device/account ownership, what was taken or transmitted, repeated conduct, age, timing, location, and whether an allegation is second-hand or uncertain. Keep the evaluation split sealed before Google Colab training.
